學語言的人每天按幾十次返回鍵，而沒有任何一個 app 在記錄那一下。 Echo 把它接住：不問問題、不打斷聆聽，只是把你重聽的那十五秒留下來，隔天再拿回來給你。 Language learners hit rewind dozens of times a day, and no app records that tap. Echo keeps the fifteen seconds you replayed — no questions, no interruption — and hands them back tomorrow.
