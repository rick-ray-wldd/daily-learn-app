自聲那條路，我出貨過I’ve shipped the own-voice piece

Mirror · AI Speech Coach 把你的回答，用你自己的克隆聲音講回來。 4 週 502 註冊、零廣告、onboarding 完成率 90.2%、191 位啟用者跑了 556 場模擬面試。 所以「克隆聲音 + 逐句串流 TTS」這條技術鏈不是投影片，是上架的東西。
但要講精確：我自己那份 N=12 的受試者內實驗裡，主觀偏好在 helpful / like-me / easiest / comfortable 四個維度全勝， 客觀指標卻沒有分離。所以自聲買到的是「願意開口」，不是療效—— Echo 的每一次驗收都回到真實原音。 Mirror · AI Speech Coach replays your spoken answer in your own cloned voice. 502 signups in four weeks, zero ad spend, 90.2% onboarding completion, 556 mock-interview sessions from 191 activated users. So the cloned-voice + per-sentence streaming TTS chain isn’t a slide — it’s shipped.
To be precise about what it proves: in my own N=12 within-subject study, subjective preference swept all four dimensions (helpful / like-me / easiest / comfortable), but the objective measures did not separate. The cloned voice buys willingness to speak, not efficacy — so Echo always grades against the original audio.
