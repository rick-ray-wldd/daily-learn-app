Why the rewind button is a good signal

3.06ODDS RATIO
Learners who spontaneously noticed a vocabulary hole were 3.06× as likely to learn that word.
4.57ODDS RATIO
Learners prompted to speak, and who noticed the gap that way: 4.57×.
n.s.BETWEEN THE TWO
The two groups did not differ significantly — forcing the action beats noticing on your own by nothing.
What does the work is realising you have a gap — not the action.
Hitting rewind is that moment. So we never interrupt.
34–38%VARIANCE EXPLAINED
Aural vocabulary + lexical segmentation explain this much of L2 listening scores. Miss one boundary, the sentence collapses.
0RIVALS COLLECTING IT
No app collects where a learner can’t even find the word boundaries. Reading apps never see it — text has no connected speech.

Noticing vocabulary holes aids incidental L2 word learning — Bilingualism: Lang. & Cognition · Field (2003), Promoting Perception: Lexical Segmentation in L2 Listening · Hulstijn & Laufer (2001), Involvement Load Hypothesis — Language Learning
